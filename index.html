A little guest guide

Good to know

When should I arrive?+

We recommend arriving by the afternoon of 4 December so you have time to settle in before the celebrations begin.

What should I pack?+

Bring festive attire for the events and a light layer for cool December evenings. Comfortable shoes are useful around the resort.

Will accommodation be available?+

Stay information and booking guidance will be shared with invited guests once arrangements are confirmed.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements?+

Absolutely. Tell us about allergies or dietary requirements when you RSVP and we will plan with care.

May I bring a plus-one or my children?+

Your invitation will indicate the guests included. If you are unsure, please get in touch with us before responding.
